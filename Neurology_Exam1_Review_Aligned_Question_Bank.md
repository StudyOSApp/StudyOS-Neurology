## WT1-001 — Additional Practice

- Topic: Spinal Anatomy / Spinal Cord Cross-Section
- Concept: Existing retrieval mapped to: Hyperreflexia in the biceps and brachioradialis
- Review objectives: No direct numbered objective
- Difficulty: Clinical/integrative
- Source: Spinal Cord Functional Organization, Lecture page not identifiable from supplemental item

Which reflex abnormality is commonly observed in patients with cervical myelopathy?

A. Absent Achilles reflex
B. Hyperreflexia in the biceps and brachioradialis
C. Normal reflex responses throughout
D. Hyporeflexia in the patellar reflex

**Correct answer: B. Hyperreflexia in the biceps and brachioradialis**

Cervical cord compression can interrupt descending upper-motor-neuron pathways that normally restrain spinal reflex circuits. Loss of that descending inhibition produces hyperreflexia below the lesion, including exaggerated biceps and brachioradialis reflexes when the relevant cervical segments are involved.
