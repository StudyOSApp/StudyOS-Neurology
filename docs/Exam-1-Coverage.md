## Interpretation of coverage

Review Objective 4 has **adequate cortical homunculus/body-map coverage**. Explanations of the factors determining disproportionate cortical representation remain **source-limited**; the course materials do not explicitly establish the requested determinant rule.

Review Objective 35 has **adequate action-potential coverage**, including resting potential, sodium/potassium permeability, threshold, depolarization, repolarization, hyperpolarization, relative refractoriness, and the sodium/potassium pump. The exact absolute-refractory mechanism in EXP-042 remains a separate source conflict, not a limitation on the entire objective.

For questions with qualified mappings, `reviewObjectives` identifies direct coverage and `supportingReviewObjectives` identifies foundational relationships that must not be counted as direct coverage. A question may support more than one objective without becoming multiple distinct questions.
