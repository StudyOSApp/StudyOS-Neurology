Course sources & slice limits

Positions follow Spinal Cord lecture diagrams, pp. 28, 30–31. The slice combines teaching landmarks from different cord levels; it is not an atlas of a single segment. Tract footprints and relay-to-tract curves are schematic. Gracilis is medial and cuneatus lateral; the latter is shown for the upper-body route.
